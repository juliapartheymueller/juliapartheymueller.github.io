Data Science @ Uni Vienna

Julia Partheymüller is a Senior Scientist at the Department of Government at the University of Vienna. She is a member of the Vienna Center for Electoral Research (VieCER), where she coordinates the survey data collection of AUTNES. She is also a member of the Research Network Data Science at the University of Vienna.

Her research focuses on comparative politics, democracy, and public opinion, with a particular emphasis on Austria, Germany, and comparative European contexts. She studies how citizens form political judgements, participate in elections, and evaluate democratic institutions and government performance. Her work addresses electoral integrity, satisfaction with democracy, and the dynamics of public opinion.

A further strand of her research examines political communication and the relationship between information, misperceptions, and political behaviour. She investigates how traditional and digital media shape citizens’ beliefs and attitudes, as well as how perceived threats relate to misinformation. Her interdisciplinary work extends to the science–politics interface, including science scepticism, trust in expertise, and public acceptance of health and crisis policies.

Methodologically, she specialises in survey research and quantitative methods, with extensive experience in large-scale data collection, survey design, survey experiments, and longitudinal analysis. She also contributes to the development of research data and digital resources for the wider scientific community.

She previously worked as a DAAD Lecturer at the University of Essex and was part of the German Longitudinal Election Study (GLES) team at the Mannheim Centre for European Social Research (MZES). She holds a doctoral degree in the Social Sciences from the University of Mannheim and studied Political Science at Free University Berlin and the University of Hamburg.
